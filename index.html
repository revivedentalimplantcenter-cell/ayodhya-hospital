About Us

Serving with care, in the heart of Karimnagar

Ayodhya Hospital brings together orthopaedic surgery and paediatric care in one trusted place. Led by experienced specialists, the hospital combines careful diagnosis, clear communication and attentive follow-up — so every patient understands their treatment and every family feels looked after.
